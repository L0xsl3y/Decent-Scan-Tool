Decent Scan Flasher

Flash the ESP32-S3 scan tool from Chrome on Android over USB (WebUSB).

1. Firmware

Loading firmware info…

2. Put the board in download mode

  1. Plug the board's USB port (native USB, not "UART") into the tablet with an OTG cable or adapter.
  2. Hold BOOT, tap RESET (RST/EN), then let go of BOOT.

3. Connect and flash

Not connected.

4. Done

When the flash finishes, tap RESET once. The board starts the new firmware. The stage-1 firmware blinks the RGB LED.
